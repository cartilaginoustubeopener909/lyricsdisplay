## ⚙️ How it Works

The application detects your active music player. It fetches lyrics from online databases. Once it finds a match, it displays the text. The software highlights words as the singer speaks them to provide a karaoke experience. 

You can pin the lyrics window to the top of your screen. This feature keeps the text visible while you work, browse the web, or play games in other windows. The application uses low system resources to ensure your computer runs smoothly.

## 🧩 Frequent Questions

**Does the software change my music player?**
No. The software only reads data from your music player. It leaves your audio files and player settings untouched.

**Is my data private?**
The application transmits data only to fetch lyrics for the specific track you play. It does not store your login credentials or listen to your microphone.

**How do I adjust the overlay?**
Click and drag the lyrics window to move it anywhere on your screen. Use the settings menu to adjust the font size, color, and transparency of the text.

**Why does the text look small?**
Open the settings menu within the application. You can scale the text size to match your screen resolution and personal preference.

**Can I use this offline?**
You need an internet connection to fetch new lyrics. Once the application loads lyrics for a song, it may cache them for later use depending on your settings.

## 🛠 Customizing the Experience

You can change how the lyrics behave from the settings menu. Open the app and look for the gear icon. Inside this menu, you can toggle the always-on-top feature. You can also change the background color of the lyrics window to ensure the text remains legible against your desktop wallpaper.

If you find that the lyrics do not match the music timing, check your playback settings. Some music players introduce a slight delay. You can adjust the sync offset in the app settings to align the lyrics with the audio. Move the slider left or right until the words match the song rhythm.
